**A list of poker terms and what they mean**

**Fundamentals**
Hand of poker - each individual round
Poker Hand - 5 card holding each player has (so your 2 cards and the flop - in texas holdem)
Hole cards/starting cards - the cards each player is given at the start of the hand
Community cards - the 5 cards in the centre of the table - also called the board
Pot - All the chips that have been bet in the current hand
Stack - the number of chips a player has to play with

**Dealing**
Deal - Distributing the cards to the players
Shuffle - Randomly order the cards before the Deal
Wash - method of shuffling, places all the cards in a pile then mixes them around and squares them
Burn - only in live poker - dealer discards a card before dealing the flop turn or river

**Blinds**
Blind Level - section of the tournament where the blinds are fixed at given amounts
Steal the Blinds - Player bets pre-flop hoping everyone else calls
Defend the Blinds - not folding pre-flop from the big or small blind position
Blinding out - when someone isn't losing any chips from playing but from the blind 
Walk - player in the big blind wins the pot because everyone else folds pre-flop

**Betting Streets**
Street - each stage of a hand where there is betting
